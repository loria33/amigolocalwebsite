AMIGOLOCAL FOR IOS & ANDROID

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains how the AmigoLocal mobile app for iOS and Android, provided by RedCalf, handles information. It applies to the app, including its chat, voice, notes, email assistant, document, and subscription features.

1. Your conversations and on-device AI

AmigoLocal runs its downloaded language models on your device. Text prompts, generated replies, text extracted from imported documents, and text you ask the model to polish are processed locally, rather than sent to an AmigoLocal AI server. You do not need an AmigoLocal account.

The app does not include advertising or analytics tracking of your conversations. This does not mean every feature operates without a network connection: downloads, purchases, external apps, and device speech services are described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores chat history, saved conversation threads, notes, preferences, downloaded models, and, if enabled, your app PIN on your device. It also stores trial and subscription status and daily feature-usage counts locally. These counts enforce the free plan’s allowances; they are not conversation analytics sent to us.

Local storage is not a promise of additional encryption. Device security, operating-system access, and any device backups you enable can affect the protection and retention of local information.

3. Microphone and speech features

Voice input requires microphone permission. On iOS, speech recognition permission is also required. The app uses a third-party speech library and the device’s speech recognition services to turn speech into text, and a bundled voice model to read text aloud.

Speech processing and offline availability depend on your operating system, speech provider, device, and language. The app does not enforce an on-device-only recognition mode, so audio may be processed by your device’s speech service under that provider’s privacy terms. You can decline or revoke these permissions in device settings and continue using text features.

4. Documents, copying, and email

When you select a supported document, the app reads its contents for local document questions. It does not upload the document to an AmigoLocal server. Selecting a file from a cloud storage provider may involve that provider downloading the file to your device.

Copy actions place selected text on your device clipboard. The email assistant lets you copy the drafted subject and body for use in another app. Information you choose to paste, share, or send outside AmigoLocal is then subject to the destination app’s practices.

5. Downloads and purchases

AI model downloads connect to Hugging Face and its delivery infrastructure. These services receive the network information needed to serve a download, such as your IP address and the requested file. Model downloads do not require sending your conversations.

Apple App Store or Google Play handles in-app purchases, payments, restoration, and subscription management. The app receives purchase information and entitlement status from the store; it does not collect your payment card details. The relevant store and download provider handle their information under their own privacy policies.

6. Your controls and retention

You can delete messages, saved threads, and notes using the app’s controls, and remove downloaded models in Settings. Clearing the current chat does not delete separately saved threads or notes; delete those separately when needed.

Removing the app or clearing its app data removes local data subject to your operating system’s behavior. Backups and files held by other apps are managed separately. Uninstalling does not cancel a store subscription. We do not hold a server copy of your local chats that we can recover or delete for you.

7. Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests, contact RedCalf at redcalf2007@gmail.com. If you email support, the email service processes your address, message, and any attachments, and we use that correspondence to respond. Share only the information needed for your request.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information, or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us about information you have sent to us; use the app and device controls for information stored only on your device.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its information practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
